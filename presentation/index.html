Walkable Basel

Team Kool Aid's pedestrian network, coloured by heat exposure

The mission

Drawing attention to where Basel's pedestrian infrastructure falls short.

Our focus: urban heat

The problem
  • Basel summers are getting hotter, and heat hits people on foot directly
  • 500 m in sun is far harder than 500 m in shade, sometimes dangerous for the elderly, children or anyone with a heart condition
  • A binding heat analysis exists (Stadtklimaanalyse), but only as a static, city-wide map
Our approach
  • Model Basel's walkable network as a graph, each edge with a cost
  • Make the hottest streets visible and explorable
Who it's for
  • City planners and administration: find heat-stressed areas and connections, compare the effect of trees and fountains
  • Politics and the public: a picture of the problem that anyone understands in seconds

Data, and our approach to it

Data
  • OpenStreetMap: ways, rivers, names, bridges & tunnels
  • Stadtklimaanalyse (geo.bs.ch): PET heat raster, 10 m
  • Tree cadastre & fountains (data.bs.ch): shade and water
  • swissALTI3D (swisstopo): 2 m terrain, for slope
Planned next: the 2030 heat scenario, already in the data.
Approach
  • Fetch from each source's API or download
  • Join every edge to PET, shade, fountains and slope
  • Store it all in one DuckDB file
  • Scripts, not notebooks: rerun end-to-end on new data

From slides to the live app

Geographic Cost space
  • Geographic view: the graph on a calm map, edges coloured by cost
  • Cost-space view: same graph, redrawn so on-screen length equals cost
  • Click a street for its cost breakdown, or an intersection to compare the streets that meet there
  • Compare routes: shortest versus coolest, and the extra strain the cool one avoids
